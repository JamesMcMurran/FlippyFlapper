Page layouts

Fit images on white paper, join a two-page spread, or add a panel that folds outward.

Auto uses the first and last images as covers when they are not wide and treats images at least 1.6× the paper width-to-height ratio as spreads. Set a wide image to Fold-out to keep a normal page beside it. Spreads may add a blank page to stay together.
