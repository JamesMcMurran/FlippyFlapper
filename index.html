Page layouts

Fit images on white paper, join a two-page spread, or add a panel that folds outward.

Auto treats portrait and square images as singles, landscape images up to twice as wide as tall as spreads, and wider images as fold-outs. The first spread wraps the front/right and back/left covers. Imposition overrides individual layout choices. Spreads may add a blank page to stay together.
